Firefly · v2 bracelet · design review

Stay connected. Go further together.

A slim bangle with a soft capsule on top: two round buttons and one tiny light. Button 1 finds your friends (press once) or calls them to you (press twice). Button 2 tells them you're home. Hold both for 3 seconds and it sends a quiet SOS.

Version 2, following the team's design board. The v1 outputs stay unchanged in the main folders.

Two buttons, three gestures

Find, call, home, and help when you need it

The buttons feel different under your thumb: Button 1 has a dot, Button 2 a line. The light between them answers back, and a small motor buzzes so you don't need to look.

Button 1 · Find friends / Come find me

  • pressFind friends. The bracelet buzzes and the light pulses faster as you get closer to your group.
  • press 2×Come find me. Your group's bracelets buzz twice and their app shows how far you are.
  • feelRound, slightly domed, with an engraved dot.

Button 2 · I'm home

  • pressTells your group you made it home. The light glows green when it's delivered.
  • press 2×Within 5 seconds: cancels, in case you pressed it by accident.
  • feelSame size, with an engraved line.

Both buttons · SOS (hidden)

  • hold 3 sA quiet SOS with your live location to your group and your backup contact. The light blinks red.
  • grace5–10 seconds to cancel by pressing either button twice, before anything is sent.
  • why bothYou can't squeeze both by accident in a bag, and nobody can see you do it.

Where everything hides

Everything fits in the capsule and the band

It needsHidden inHow it works
The lightA pinhole between the buttonsOne RGB LED under a 1.2 mm sapphire window: pink for finding, amber for come find me, green for home, red for SOS.
Brains and radiosThe capsuleA stadium-shaped board with Bluetooth, ultra-wideband (UWB) for distance, LoRa for watch-free range, and LTE-M cellular + GPS for 6 miles and more.
AntennasThe band itselfThe metal band, split by an insulating gap at the hinge, works as the LoRa and cellular antenna. UWB and Bluetooth leave through the ceramic underside of the capsule.
BatteryCapsule + claspAn 85 mAh cell in the capsule and a 60 mAh cell in the slim clasp under the wrist, about 145 mAh together.
Buzz and chargingUnder the capsuleA coin motor against the wrist and two magnetic charge pins on the underside.
Range · at least 6 miles, even with a weak signal

Close, near and far: three ways to stay connected

No bracelet-sized radio reaches 6 miles directly, so it picks the best link for how far apart you are.

DistanceLinkWhat you get
0–30 mUWB, bracelet to braceletDistance to 10–30 cm with a clear path; reliable to about 10–20 m in a crowd. Powers Find friends and the separation alert.
30 m – 1 kmLoRa, bracelet to braceletCome find me, I'm home and SOS with no phone, tower or internet; 50–150 m in a packed club, more with relays.
1 km – 6+ milesCellular LTE-M / NB-IoTGPS position, I'm home and SOS from anywhere with coverage; about 20 dB more reach than a phone's normal LTE in basements. Fallbacks: Amazon Sidewalk and passing phones.
