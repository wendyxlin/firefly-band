AFTERGLOW / V2 APP

Stay close.
Go out.

The companion app for the Afterglow watch. One friend with battery runs it; everyone else just wears the watch, which has two buttons: the crown says I'm Home, the red pusher sends SOS.

  1. 1 Start your night-out circle.
  2. 2 Simulate a friend leaving the buddy radius.
  3. 3 Find a friend or send Come Find Me.
  4. 4 Press the watch crown for I'm Home, or hold the pusher for SOS (Settings → Watch).

Install it: open this page on your phone, then Share → Add to Home Screen (iPhone) or ⋮ → Install app (Android). Distances and alerts are simulated until a watch is paired.
