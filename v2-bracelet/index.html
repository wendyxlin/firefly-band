FAQ

Questions

When does it ship?

The first batch is still being made by our student team at NYU Stern. Add Firefly to your cart to reserve your place on the notify list; nothing is charged.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. A small amount of cellular data is bundled for the bracelet's lifetime. Location messages are tiny.

Does it work if my phone is dead?

Yes. Find friends, come find me, I'm home and SOS all go out from the bracelet itself.

Does everyone in my group need one?

Friends without a bracelet can join the group in the app and see check-ins and SOS alerts. Distance finding needs a bracelet on both wrists, which is why packs are cheaper.

Where is the SOS button?

There isn't a separate one. Hold both buttons for 3 seconds. It can't happen by accident in a bag, and nobody around you can tell. You get 5–10 seconds to cancel.

How long does the battery last?

About 9 hours on a heavy night with lots of finding, and more than a day on a quiet one.
