Afterglow, worn as a watch

A dress watch with exactly two buttons. The crown sends I'm Home, the red-ringed pusher is SOS. Everything else from the Afterglow app happens on its own: the watch buzzes when you drift outside your buddy radius, and its mother-of-pearl dial glows to show where your circle is.

Two buttons

One to say you're home, one for help

Each button feels different, so you can find the right one by touch in the dark. Finding friends and "Come Find Me" moved into the app, as in the Afterglow demo, so the watch only needs these two.

Crown at 3 o'clock · I'm Home

  • pressSends "I'm Home" to your circle. Two short buzzes confirm it, then a green sweep around the dial when it's delivered.
  • press againWithin 5 seconds: cancels, in case you pressed it by accident.
  • feelKnurled gold crown with a lavender opal on the tip.

Pusher at 2 o'clock · SOS

  • hold 3 sSOS to your whole circle and your backup contact. The dial pulses red until someone acknowledges.
  • press 2×Cancels during the 5–10 second grace window, before anything is sent.
  • feelSmooth, slimmer than the crown, ringed in red enamel. Recessed so a bent wrist can't press it.

Where everything hides

Every Afterglow part has a watch part to hide behind

Afterglow needsHidden asHow it works
The glowMother-of-pearl dial, lit markersTwelve LEDs shine through a small translucent window under each hour marker: lavender toward your circle, green for I'm Home, red for SOS.
AntennasInside the strapFlexible antennas laminated in the leather, outside the metal case: LoRa on one side, cellular and GPS on the other.
Close-range distanceUnder the dialAn ultra-wideband (UWB) chip measures distance to nearby friends to about 10–30 cm, which powers the buddy radius and "about 8 m away".
Long rangeOne chip on the boardCellular LTE-M / NB-IoT with built-in GPS (Nordic nRF9151) for 6 miles and beyond, with Amazon Sidewalk and phone crowd networks as fallbacks.
BatteryUnder the dialA round cell about 30 mm across and 3.3 mm thick, about 210 mAh.
Vibration and chargingThe casebackA coin motor against the skin, and two magnetic charge pins.
Telling timeReal handsA small stepper driven by the same chip, like a hybrid smartwatch.
Range · at least 6 miles, even with a weak signal

Close, near and far: three ways to stay connected

No wrist-sized radio reaches 6 miles directly, so the watch picks the best link for how far apart you are.

DistanceLinkWhat you get
0–30 mUWB, watch to watchDistance to within 10–30 cm when there's a clear path; bodies in a crowd block it, so it falls back to LoRa signal strength. Powers the buddy radius.
30 m – 1 kmLoRa, watch to watchSeparation alerts, Come Find Me and SOS with no phone, tower or internet; 50–150 m in a packed club, more with relays.
1 km – 6+ milesCellular LTE-M / NB-IoTGPS position every 1–2 minutes, I'm Home and SOS from anywhere with coverage; about 20 dB more reach than a phone's normal LTE in basements. Fallbacks: Amazon Sidewalk and passing phones.
