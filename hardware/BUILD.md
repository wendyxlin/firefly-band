# Building a Firefly band (prototype)

About **$55 in parts per band** (see [`BOM.csv`](BOM.csv)). The first one takes an afternoon, and each one after that under three hours. Build at least two so you can test band to band.

## Tools
- Temperature-controlled soldering iron (fine tip), solder, flux pen
- Tweezers, 30 AWG wire strippers, flush cutters, multimeter
- 3D printer (resin is best; 0.12 mm FDM works) or a print service (Craftcloud, JLC3DP)
- E6000 or hot glue, UV resin pen, USB-C cable, laptop with Arduino IDE 2.x

## Small parts (one kit covers ~10 bands)
AO3400 MOSFET (SOT-23), 1N4148 diode, resistors 10 kΩ ×2 / 3.3 kΩ / 15 kΩ / 330 Ω / 100 Ω, 47 µF capacitor, 1.5 mm low-profile 2.54 mm headers, JST-PH 2-pin, a 26 × 4 mm perfboard strip, 30 AWG silicone wire, Kapton tape, 1 mm foam tape, 1 mm O-ring cord, M1.6 × 6 screws + heat-set inserts.

## Wiring

| Signal | XIAO pin | Connects to | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| SPI SCK / MISO / MOSI | D8 / D9 / D10 | Wio-SX1262 (stacked header) | Don't reuse |
| LoRa chip select | D4 | SX1262 NSS | |
| LoRa interrupt | D1 | SX1262 DIO1 | |
| LoRa reset / busy | D2 / D3 | SX1262 RESET / BUSY | |
| RF switch | D5 | SX1262 antenna switch | HIGH before using the radio |
| Buttons | D0 (A0) | 10 kΩ pull-up to 3V3; Button 1 → 3.3 kΩ → GND; Button 2 → 15 kΩ → GND | idle ≈3.3 V, B2 ≈2.0 V, B1 ≈0.8 V |
| Motor | D6 | 100 Ω to MOSFET gate, 10 kΩ gate→GND; motor 3V3 → drain; 1N4148 across motor (stripe to 3V3); 47 µF across 3V3/GND | |
| LEDs | D7 | 330 Ω → SK6812 #1 DIN, chain DOUT→DIN; VDD 3V3, GND | |
| Battery | BAT+ / BAT− pads (underside) | 150 mAh LiPo 502020 via JST | Charger is on the XIAO |
| Charging | 5V / GND pads | magnetic pogo receptacle | |

## Steps
1. **Print the shells:** `firefly_shell_bottom.stl` and `firefly_shell_lid.stl` (44 × 24 × 11 mm closed, 1.2 mm walls, 41.6 × 21.6 × 8.6 mm inside). They're generated by `make_stl.py`, so change the numbers there and re-run if you need to.
2. **Flash Blink on the bare XIAO first** to confirm the board works.
3. **Stack the radio:** low-profile headers, LoRa module underneath, XIAO on top with USB-C up, ~1.5 mm gap. Trim pins flush. Click the antenna onto U.FL.
4. **Button ladder** on a 20 × 6 mm perfboard scrap. Verify voltages on D0 with the multimeter.
5. **Motor driver:** MOSFET, flyback diode, cap. Glue the motor on top of the battery.
6. **LED strip:** four SK6812 Mini-E 6 mm apart. Test with NeoPixel `strandtest`.
7. **Battery and charging:** JST to BAT pads (red = +, check twice), pogo leads to 5V/GND, Kapton around the cell.
8. **Dry fit:** boards left, battery and motor right, LEDs under the lid slot, antenna on foam along the back of the lid, buttons against the front wall. Stack must be ≤ 8.5 mm.
9. **Flash `firmware/firefly/firefly.ino`** to two bands. Double-press on one should buzz the other within a second. Walk one down the hall to check range.
10. **Measure current** in series with the battery: under ~5 mA average idle with LoRa listening.
11. **Close up:** glue the diffuser, seal button edges with UV resin, seat the O-ring, screw together, thread the cord.
12. **Pair:** hold Button 1 for 5 s on every band together. They buzz twice when they share the group key. Pair the app from *My band*.

## Safety
- LiPo: don't puncture, keep the protection board, charge only through the XIAO.
- Never transmit without the antenna connected.
- 915 MHz is license-free for hobby use in the US; selling needs FCC Part 15 testing. Use 868 MHz in the EU.
