# Building a Firefly band (prototype)

About **$76 in parts per band** (see [`BOM.csv`](BOM.csv)). The first one takes an afternoon, and each one after that under three hours. Build at least two so you can test band to band.

## Tools
- Temperature-controlled soldering iron (fine tip), solder, flux pen
- Tweezers, 30 AWG wire strippers, flush cutters, multimeter
- 3D printer (resin is best; 0.12 mm FDM works) or a print service (Craftcloud, JLC3DP)
- E6000 or hot glue, UV resin pen, USB-C cable, laptop with Arduino IDE 2.x

## Small parts (one kit covers ~10 bands)
AO3400 MOSFET (SOT-23), 1N4148 diode, resistors 10 kΩ ×2 / 3.3 kΩ / 15 kΩ / 330 Ω / 100 Ω, 47 µF capacitor, 1.5 mm low-profile 2.54 mm headers, JST-PH 2-pin, a 13 × 3 mm flex strip, 2 low-profile 3 × 3 × 1.5 mm tactile switches, 2 Ø3.8 mm gold beads (one with a tiny set stone), a 15 × 11 mm opalite cabochon, a 6 mm hinged gold-plated bangle, 34 AWG enamelled wire, a gold electroplating kit, 30 AWG silicone wire, Kapton tape, 1 mm foam tape, 1 mm O-ring cord, a bezel pusher.

## Wiring

| Signal | XIAO pin | Connects to | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| SPI SCK / MISO / MOSI | D8 / D9 / D10 | Wio-SX1262 (stacked header) | Don't reuse |
| LoRa chip select | D4 | SX1262 NSS | |
| LoRa interrupt | D1 | SX1262 DIO1 | |
| LoRa reset / busy | D2 / D3 | SX1262 RESET / BUSY | |
| RF switch | D5 | SX1262 antenna switch | HIGH before using the radio |
| Buttons | D0 (A0) | 10 kΩ pull-up to 3V3; Button 1 → 3.3 kΩ → GND; Button 2 → 15 kΩ → GND | idle ≈3.3 V, B2 ≈2.0 V, B1 ≈0.8 V |
| Motor | D6 | 100 Ω to MOSFET gate, 10 kΩ gate→GND; motor 3V3 → drain; 1N4148 across motor (stripe to 3V3); 47 µF across 3V3/GND | |
| LEDs | D7 | 330 Ω → SK6812 #1 DIN, chain DOUT→DIN; VDD 3V3, GND | |
| Battery | BAT+ / BAT− pads (underside) | 110 mAh LiPo 401230 in the clasp box, via 34 AWG wires through the bangle | Charger is on the XIAO |
| Charging | 5V / GND pads | magnetic charge pins on the clasp box | |

## Steps
1. **Print and plate the centerpiece, wings and clasp:** `firefly_centerpiece_base.stl`, `firefly_centerpiece_dome.stl`, `firefly_wing.stl` (print two) and `firefly_clasp_box.stl`. The centerpiece is a 28 × 24 × 11 mm cushion that rolls under toward the wrist (1.0 mm walls), with two Art Deco tiers stepping up to an oval halo: 26 seats for 1.2 mm white stones and a bezel collar over a light window for the 15 × 11 mm stone. Each end has two angled Ø2.4 mm sockets for the band's wires and a hole for a wing's switch post. The capsule clasp is 35 × 14 × 8 mm with matching wire sockets in both ends. Print in resin at 0.05 mm, sand to 1500 grit, apply conductive paint, and electroplate copper then 18k gold (or use a plating service). The files are generated by `make_stl.py`, so change the numbers there and re-run if you need to.
2. **Flash Blink on the bare XIAO first** to confirm the board works.
3. **Stack the radio:** low-profile headers, LoRa module underneath, XIAO on top, ~1.5 mm gap. Trim pins flush. Click the compact antenna onto U.FL and curve it along the inside back wall of the centerpiece.
4. **Wing buttons:** two 3 × 3 × 1.5 mm tactile switches on small Kapton-insulated standoffs at the two ends of the centerpiece, 24 mm apart under the actuator holes; push each wing's post through its hole onto a plunger. Set a small white stone in the right wing (Button 2) so the two feel different. Wire the resistor ladder and check the voltages on D0.
5. **Motor driver:** MOSFET, flyback diode, cap. The 8 mm motor goes in the clasp box, on top of the battery, against the skin.
6. **LED strip:** four SK6812 Mini-E 3.5 mm apart on a 13 mm strip, centred under the stone. Test with NeoPixel `strandtest`.
7. **Battery and charging (clasp box):** run four 34 AWG enamelled wires through the hollow bangle (with a slack loop at the hinge): BAT+/BAT− to the battery's JST plug and 5V/GND to the charge pins. Red is +, check twice. Kapton around the cell.
8. **Dry fit:** board stack in the centerpiece; battery, motor and charge pins in the clasp box. The board stack must be ≤ 8 mm.
9. **Flash `firmware/firefly/firefly.ino`** to two bangles. Double-press on one should buzz the other within a second. Walk one down the hall to check range.
10. **Measure current** in series with the battery: under ~5 mA average idle with LoRa listening.
11. **Set the stones and close up:** drop the opalite into the bezel collar and burnish the bezel over it, set the 26 halo stones with a drop of UV resin each, seal under the wings with silicone, seat the gasket and snap the dome onto the base around the bangle. Snap the clasp box closed.
12. **Fit the bangle:** push the ends of the double-wire bangle into the sockets in the centerpiece and the clasp, and glue them once the battery leads are routed through one wire. A 62 × 48 mm inside oval fits most 155–175 mm wrists.
13. **Pair:** hold Button 1 for 5 s on every bangle together. They buzz twice when they share the group key. Pair the app from *My band*.

## Safety
- LiPo: don't puncture, keep the protection board, charge only through the XIAO.
- Never transmit without the antenna connected.
- 915 MHz is license-free for hobby use in the US; selling needs FCC Part 15 testing. Use 868 MHz in the EU.
