The film

1:47 AM. Your phone just died.

Why we're building it

Every night out, the same three problems.

We interviewed NYU juniors and seniors who go out every week. These came up again and again.

Phones die

Photos, Maps and location sharing drain the battery. The friend with 0% is the one nobody can reach.

No signal, no sightline

Basements kill GPS and cell service, and a shared location can't help you spot someone in a dark, packed room.

Groups split

Drinks, restrooms, Ubers. One person ends up coordinating, and someone gets home without anyone knowing.

How it works

Two gold wings. Everything you need in the dark.

No screen, no app to open. Feel for the wings on either side of the stone and press. The Safety wing carries a small stone, so you can tell them apart by touch.

Button 1 · Find

Hot or cold

  • 1×Find your friends. The bangle pulses faster and the stone glows brighter as you get closer.
  • 2×"Come find me" to the group.
  • hold"I'm okay" check-in.
Button 2 · Safety

Help, quietly

  • 1×A discreet "I need help" to the friends you choose. No light, one small buzz.
  • 2×Cancel an accidental alert.
  • 3 sSOS to every band nearby, with your distance and direction.
Button 1 · 3×

I'm home

  • 3×Friends still out get "Jordan is home" right away.
  • textYour phone texts Mom or your roommate as soon as it's charging, even if it died on the way.
  • autoOr turn on "text when I arrive home" and skip the button.
Works without your phone

No battery. No signal. Still connected.

Every band carries its own long-range radio. Bands talk to each other directly, through walls and crowds, with nothing in between.

  • LoRa radio, 915 MHz: band to band, 300 m+ indoors.
  • Bluetooth: precise finding within 25 m, plus the link to whichever friend still has battery and the app.
  • Private: no GPS on the band, a group key that expires at sunrise, and nothing stored on a server.
Designed like jewelry

Fine jewelry first. A tracker never.

A cushion-shaped centerpiece that steps up in Art Deco tiers to a halo of small white stones around an opalite that glows only when it needs to. Two gold firefly wings are the buttons, a slim twin-wire band sweeps around the wrist, and the battery hides in the clasp.

The app

One phone guides everyone.

Whoever still has battery sees the whole group on a map, gets every alert, follows a compass straight to anyone, and sees who made it home. Try it now: it runs a demo night until you have a band.

Open the Firefly app

Install it on your phone

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open wendyxlin.github.io/firefly-band/app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen → Add.
  3. Open Firefly from your home screen. It runs full-screen and works offline.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the same link in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install app when it pops up (or ⋮ → Add to Home screen).
  3. On Android you can also pair a real band over Bluetooth.

FAQ

Questions we get a lot.

Does my phone need to be on?

No. Finding, check-ins, help alerts and SOS all run band to band over radio. One friend's phone with the app adds the map and alert history, but it's optional.

Can anyone track me?

The band has no GPS and stores no location. It only knows that a band with tonight's group key is nearby. The key is created when your group taps bands together and is wiped at the end of the night. Nothing goes to a server.

How does "I'm home" work if my phone died?

Triple-press Button 1. Friends whose bands are in range hear it right away. Your band also holds the message, and the moment your phone is charging and near the band, the app texts the people you picked.

Is it waterproof?

It's splash-proof: spilled drinks and rain are fine, and it charges magnetically through the clasp so there's no open port. Don't swim with it.

How far apart can friends be?

Precise hot-or-cold finding works within about 25 m. Alerts and check-ins reach 300 m or more indoors, and over a kilometre outdoors.
