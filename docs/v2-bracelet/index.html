Why it exists

The group chat goes quiet right when it matters.

We interviewed NYU students who go out often. The same three things came up every time.

"My phone died and I couldn't find anyone."

Phones die, signal drops in basements, and texts arrive twenty minutes late.

"Text me when you're home."

Everyone says it. Half the group forgets, and one friend ends up chasing everyone.

"I didn't want to make a scene."

Unlocking a phone to ask for help is obvious. Holding two buttons on a bracelet isn't.

Range

Six miles and more, even with one bar.

The bracelet picks the best way to reach your group. It doesn't need your phone to send I'm home or SOS.

Design

Minimal. Stylish. Meaningful.

A 3.2 mm band and a soft capsule on top. The radios and battery hide inside the capsule and a slim clasp under the wrist; the band itself is the antenna.

Privacy

Shared for the night, then gone.

Only your group

Location goes to the people you added tonight. There is no public map and no follower list.

Ends with the night

The group key expires at the end of the night and sharing stops.

Encrypted on the bracelet

Positions are encrypted before they leave, so the networks that carry them can't read them.

FAQ

Questions

Where is the SOS button?

There isn't a separate one. Hold both buttons for 3 seconds. That can't happen by accident in a bag, and nobody around you can tell. You get 5–10 seconds to cancel by pressing either button twice.

What if I press I'm home by accident?

Press Button 2 twice within 5 seconds and it's cancelled before anyone is told.

Does it work if my phone is dead?

Yes. Find friends, come find me, I'm home and SOS all go out from the bracelet itself, over its own radio or cellular connection.

How far away can my friends find me?

Up close (0–30 m) the app shows a distance to the metre. Across a venue or a few blocks it uses bracelet-to-bracelet radio. Beyond a kilometre, built-in cellular covers six miles and more, and it is designed for weak, one-bar signal.

Is there a monthly fee?

No. A small amount of cellular data is bundled for the bracelet's lifetime. Location messages are tiny.

How long does the battery last?

About 9 hours on a heavy night with lots of finding and cellular check-ins, and more than a day on a quiet one. A magnetic charger snaps onto the underside.

Does everyone in my group need one?

Friends without a bracelet can join the group in the app and see check-ins and SOS alerts. Distance finding and bracelet-to-bracelet radio need a bracelet on both wrists.
