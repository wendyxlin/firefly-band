FIREFLY / V2 APP

Stay connected.
Go further together.

The companion app for the Firefly bracelet. Create a group, check on your friends, and see who's made it home. The bracelet has two buttons: Button 1 finds friends (press) or calls them to you (press twice), Button 2 says I'm home. Hold both for 3 seconds for SOS.

  1. 1 Start your group for the night.
  2. 2 Find a friend: "Getting closer… 3 meters away".
  3. 3 Simulate a friend drifting outside the radius.
  4. 4 Try the bracelet's buttons in Settings → Bracelet.

Install it: open this page on your phone, then Share → Add to Home Screen (iPhone) or ⋮ → Install app (Android). Distances and alerts are simulated until a bracelet is paired.
