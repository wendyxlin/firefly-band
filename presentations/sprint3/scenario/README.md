# 15% checkout scenario (illustrative, not test data)

These charts are a made-up what-if for internal use: 32 of 213 visitors clicking checkout (15%) instead of the real 9 of 213 (4.2%).
They are NOT PostHog or shop-log data. The real charts are in `../charts`, and the real numbers are in `../data`.

Scenario numbers: by price $129 7 of 74, $169 11 of 64, $199 14 of 75; by channel paid 11 of 148, organic 18 of 43, other 3 of 22; waitlist 14.
